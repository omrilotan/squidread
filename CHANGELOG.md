# Changelog

Releases of the Android app. Each entry's notes are what goes into Google Play's "Release notes" (500 characters at most).

## 1.0.0-rc-20261019 (versionCode 5)

- Books without a table of contents now list their sections in Contents, named after each section's heading, so you can jump between them.

## 1.0.0-rc-20261018 (versionCode 4)

- A new first screen, in place of the hello: where you read, how to turn pages, and how to get your first book. With books but none open, a "Select a book" button opens the library.
- Suggestions to get started: free books from Project Gutenberg in an empty library, and a font to add, with a how-to, in an empty fonts folder.
- Bug fix: some EPUBs that list the same file twice failed to open. They now open.

## 1.0.0-rc-20261017 (versionCode 3)

- A short hello on first launch, with a tip on making Squidread your default EPUB reader. An empty library shows the same tip.
- Tidier, more compact menu.
- Bug fix: with a large display or font size, menu tab names broke across two lines. They now shrink to fit.

## 1.0.0-rc-20261016 (versionCode 2)

- Dark theme: white on black. Choose System, Light or Dark in Settings; System follows your device.
- New app icon.
- Bug fix: in right-to-left books, a middle "Next" tap zone turned to the previous page. The tap zone grid is now mirrored, so a "Next" zone always turns to the next page.

## 1.0.0-rc-20261015 (versionCode 1)

First release on Google Play.

- Pure black on white, instant page turns, no animations
- Right-to-left books turn pages the right way
- Choose what each tap zone does; page buttons supported
- Use your own fonts
- Footnotes open over the page
- No internet access, accounts, ads or tracking
