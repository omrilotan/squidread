Squidread

A fast, distraction-free EPUB reader built for e-ink.

Download for Android Open the web reader

The Android app needs Android 7 or newer. The web reader works in any modern browser and can be installed as an app.

The Android app

Made for paper-like screens

  • Black and white

    Pure black on white. Instant page turns, no animations.

  • Pages turn the book's way

    Right-to-left books, like Hebrew, Arabic and manga, turn right to left.

  • Controls your way

    Tap, swipe or page buttons. You choose what each tap zone does.

  • Private by design

    No internet, accounts, ads or tracking. Your books stay on your device. Privacy policy

  • Your default reader

    Set it once, and every EPUB you open goes straight to Squidread. How to

  • One book, one window

    Each open book gets its own entry in the app switcher.
